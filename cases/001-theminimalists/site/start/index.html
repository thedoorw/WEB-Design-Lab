Questions the archive can answer

How is the work made?

Process entries can explain tools, constraints, mistakes, and corrections without turning every project into a formal case study.

What ideas repeat?

Tags and small index pages can reveal recurring interests—geometry, typography, systems, observation—without changing the reading shell.

What belongs together?

Collections can group related experiments while individual entries remain readable in sequence.
